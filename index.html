    Asset progression

    Let the home you own fund your next one

    Most of my clients are HDB upgraders and EC owners planning their next purchase in the $2.5 million to $3 million range. I've made the same journey myself, so we start with your finances and long-term goals, then work out the order of moves that fits.

    How one of my earliest clients moved up

    1. Sold $850,000 Their five-room HDB flat, for close to that
    2. Bought $1.25 million A resale Executive Condo
    3. Today $1.5 million+ What the EC is now valued at, an increase of about $300,000
    4. Next $1.7–1.8 million Decoupling the EC and adding a new-launch condo for investment

    If their plans go ahead, they'll hold more than $3 million in property, starting from one HDB flat.
